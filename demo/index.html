How this would work

Sourcing
Community owners are added by hand or by spreadsheet, then ranked by how active, how big and how on-topic their community is.
Outreach
Claude drafts a short, personal first message for each top match, and a person reviews, edits and sends it by hand.
Onboarding
Owners who say yes get a partner link, a short welcome guide and a simple first challenge to share with their members.
Milestones and rewards
Partners unlock rewards as their referrals grow, with 10 referrals a month as the main goal.
Slack community
Signed partners join a private Slack to share wins, swap ideas and hear about new features first.
